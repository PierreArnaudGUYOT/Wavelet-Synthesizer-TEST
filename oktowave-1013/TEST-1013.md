# OktoWave 1.0.13 — test copy of the web app (not a release)

Open: https://pierrearnaudguyot.github.io/Wavelet-Synthesizer-TEST/oktowave-1013/

What to try:

- **PLAY card: VELOCITY** (new). Play a MIDI keyboard softly and hard: the volume follows the velocity.
  The knob sets how much (default 50 %: a velocity of 64 is about 8 dB softer than 127, 32 about 16 dB);
  the menu under it chooses where velocity goes: VOLUME (default), FILTER (the cutoff of the FILTER card;
  it switches the filter in, so a bright preset is a little darker even at full velocity) or VOL + FILTER.
  It is a playing setting like the tuning: changing preset keeps it.
- **EFFECTS**: SATURATION, CHORUS, PING-PONG DELAY and REVERB are written in the accent colour (they were grey).
- **Oscillator tabs**: OSC1, OSC2, OSC3, with SUBTRACTIVE / ADDITIVE written bigger.
- The demo (▶ DEMO) plays as before (VELOCITY is off during the demo, its levels were set without it).

The plug-in of this version also has **▶ PLAY DEMO** left of SAVE PRESET (not in this web copy: the web app has its own ▶ DEMO).
