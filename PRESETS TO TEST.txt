WAVELET SYNTHESIZER TEST - presets to listen to
=============================================

Plugin: "Wavelet Synthesizer TEST" (installs next to the official Wavelet Synthesizer).
Every preset name starts with its number: note the numbers you like, they will be copied into the official version.
A = engine A (subtractive), B = engine B (additive), AB = both engines mixed.
"Wave" in a name = a preset that exploits the wavelets (morph, sparsity, position window, Haar...).

NEW: WAVELET MORPH (engine A panel: WAVELET 2, MORPH, ENV>WAV; LFO section: WAVELET).
The sound is also built with WAVELET 2 and the two are crossfaded: MORPH + LFO x "LFO > WAVELET" + mod envelope x ENV>WAV.
In engine A the wavelet is heard where the sound is shaped in the wavelet domain: scale filter (cutoff, resonance),
sparsity, position window. Presets 101-150 use the morph; many move the morph and the cutoff at the same time.
